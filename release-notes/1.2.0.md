## New in 1.2.0

- **Updates itself.** World Clock+ checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, click **Update** in the menu bar panel and it installs and reopens, keeping your cities and settings. You can also check, or turn the daily check off, in **Settings › Updates**.

**Upgrading from 1.1.x:** install 1.2.0 the usual way, by replacing the app in Applications. Later versions then come to you.
