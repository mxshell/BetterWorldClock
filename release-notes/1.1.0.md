## New in 1.1.0

- **New name.** The app is now called **World Clock+**. The project is still Better World Clock; that name is too long to show in full under an app icon.
- **New logo.** A globe of dots, half in daylight, is the app icon and the menu bar mark. It replaces the TIME wordmark.

**Upgrading from 1.0.x:** the app used to be called "World Clocks". After you install World Clock+, quit the old app and delete "World Clocks" from Applications; otherwise both will run. Your cities and settings carry over. Then check that **Launch at login** is still on in Settings.
